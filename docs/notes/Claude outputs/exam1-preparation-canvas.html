MBA 775 · Exam 1

Exam 1 Preparation

Video walkthroughs, past exams with solution guides, and worked homework problems, all in one place.

How to use this page:

  1. Take a past exam cold. Start with the Fall 2022 practice exam or any guide below. Cover the solutions, set a timer, and work the problems on your own.
  2. Check your work against the solution guide, and mark every question you missed or guessed on.
  3. Watch the walkthrough videos for the exams that have them, focusing on the questions you missed.
  4. Fill the gaps with the worked problems at the bottom of the page.
